Privacy

Last updated November 19, 2026.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, a securely hashed password if you set one, and which sign-in provider (Google or Apple) you use. From Google and Apple we receive only an account ID and your email address.
  • Your Clams: the long URL, any title or custom name you add, and when it was made.
  • Clicks: when a Clam is opened we record the time, the referring website's domain, an approximate country, the device type, browser and operating system. We do not store IP addresses. We keep a one-way, keyed hash so we can count unique visitors without knowing who they are.

Cookies

We use a sign-in cookie, a security cookie that protects forms, and (if you shorten links without an account) a cookie that lets you save those Clams to an account later. No advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data. Clam owners see aggregate stats for their own Clams. Anyone can see where a Clam points (that's how short links work).

The browser extension

The extension only reads the address and title of the tab you're on, and only when you click it. It sends them to cla.ms to make a Clam. It doesn't read page contents or your browsing history.

Deleting your data

You can delete any Clam, or your whole account, from your account page at any time.

Country lookup uses IP Geolocation by DB-IP.
