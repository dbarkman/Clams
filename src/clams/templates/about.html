About Clams 🐚

Clams is a small, friendly link shortener. A short link on cla.ms is called a Clam — because, well, why not?

What you get

  • Short links like {{ settings.short_host }}/aB3xY9. Codes are case-sensitive, so copy and paste them rather than retyping.
  • No account needed. With a free account you can edit where a Clam goes, switch it off, pick custom names, and see click stats.
  • Click stats: clicks over time, unique visitors, referrers, countries, devices and browsers. Link-preview bots aren't counted.
  • A QR code for every Clam, a one-click browser extension, CSV export, and a simple API.
  • Add a + to the end of any Clam (like {{ settings.short_host }}/aB3xY9+) to see where it goes before you visit.

What we don't do

No ads, no interstitial pages, no selling your data, and Clams don't expire.
